## Phase 0: Survival (W1-4)

**Goal:** A1 → A1+. Build daily habit. Master core verb "to be" and Present Simple basics.

### Weekly breakdown

**W1 (May 15-21):** Alphabet & sounds, personal info, numbers 1-100, verb *to be* (am/is/are), basic 100 words (greetings, family, work).

**W2 (May 22-28):** *Have got*, articles a/an basics, plurals, 200 more words (food, time, places).

**W3 (May 29 - June 4):** Present Simple affirmative, daily routines, prepositions of time (at/in/on).

**W4 (June 5-11):** Present Simple negative + questions, *can/can't* (ability), 500 words total cumulative.

### Daily structure (90 min)

- 10 min — Speaking (ChatGPT Voice on A1 prompts)
- 20 min — Listening (BBC LearnEnglish Level 1, EnglishClass101 Level 1)
- 20 min — Vocab/Anki (10 new / 60 review)
- 25 min — Grammar/Writing (topic of the week)
- 15 min — Pronunciation (TH /θ/ /ð/, W vs V, /æ/, schwa /ə/)

## SRS rules

1. **Anki daily** — non-negotiable, even on rest days do reviews only
2. **Friday** — weekly review: grammar topic of the week + errors from writing
3. **Every 4 weeks** — month review: re-test previous grammar, reactivate forgotten cards
4. **Day 1/3/7/14/30 intervals** for new concepts (vocab + grammar)
5. **Every 2 weeks** — re-record 60-sec "tell me about yourself" Loom

## Russian L1 → English error focus

Drill on every speaking session:

1. Articles a/an/the — missing or wrong
2. Present Perfect vs Past Simple
3. Prepositions: *depends on* (not from), *on Monday* (not in), *arrive at/in* (not to)
4. *make* vs *do* collocations
5. *say* vs *tell*
6. Pronunciation: TH (/θ/, /ð/), W vs V, /æ/, schwa /ə/, ship vs sheep, no final devoicing
