# Writing — Day 7 (2026-05-21)

**Task:** 5 sentences about work and family using new vocabulary.

## Draft
I am have sister, she live in Ukraine. My wife works at home. Today i have meeting, we speak about new project. My manager say it is very interesting project.

## Corrected
I have a sister. She lives in Ukraine. My wife works at home. Today I have a meeting. We are speaking about a new project. My manager says it is a very interesting project.

## Lessons
- Articles a/an before singular nouns
- 3rd person -s on he/she/it verbs
- No "to be" before another main verb
