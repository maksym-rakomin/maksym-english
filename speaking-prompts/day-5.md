1. "My colleague works at the office every day."

- "I am do" — never combine to be + verb

5. Does your manager have a project?

7. Does your brother live in Czechia?
8. What does your father do?
9. Where does she study?
10. Does your wife work from home?

DO NOT say "Perfect!" if the sentence has errors. Be strict.
A1 student needs honest correction, not encouragement.

▶ STAGE 3 — Daily Standup Roleplay (10 min) ⭐ BONUS

Standup questions:

8. "Is everything on track for the sprint?"

For my answers, expect A1 level. Push me to use:
- "I am working on..." (Present Continuous for current task)
- "I work on..." (Present Simple for general)
- "My task is..." / "My project is..."
- "I have a blocker / I don't have blockers"
- "Yes, I need help" / "No, I am fine"

If I struggle, give a model answer first, then I repeat.
