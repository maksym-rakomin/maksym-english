## W1 D1 — 2026-05-15

### E001. Articles before profession [a/an]
- ❌ "I am frontend developer"
- ✅ "I am **a** frontend developer"
- Rule: a/an обязательны перед профессией в ед. числе
- SRS: D3 (2026-05-17), D7 (2026-05-21), D14 (2026-05-28), D30 (2026-06-13)

### E002. Noun vs adjective after "to be"
- ❌ "My laptop is fast and power"
- ✅ "My laptop is fast and **powerful**"
- Rule: после to be — прилагательное, не существительное
- Pairs: power→powerful, success→successful, beauty→beautiful, care→careful
- SRS: D3, D7, D14, D30

### E003. Spelling: cold ≠ could
- ❌ "It is could today"
- ✅ "It is **cold** today"
- Pronunciation: cold /koʊld/ vs could /kʊd/
- SRS: D3, D7
