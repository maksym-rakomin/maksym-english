## W1 D1 — 2026-05-15

### E001. Articles before profession [a/an]
- ❌ "I am frontend developer"
- ✅ "I am **a** frontend developer"
- Rule: a/an обязательны перед профессией в ед. числе
- SRS: D3 (2026-05-17), D7 (2026-05-21), D14 (2026-05-28), D30 (2026-06-13)

### E002. Noun vs adjective after "to be"
- ❌ "My laptop is fast and power"
- ✅ "My laptop is fast and **powerful**"
- Rule: после to be — прилагательное, не существительное
- Pairs: power→powerful, success→successful, beauty→beautiful, care→careful
- SRS: D3, D7, D14, D30

### E003. Spelling: cold ≠ could
- ❌ "It is could today"
- ✅ "It is **cold** today"
- Pronunciation: cold /koʊld/ vs could /kʊd/
- SRS: D3, D7

## W1 D2 — 2026-05-16

### E004. Article in questions too
- ❌ "Are you developer?" → ✅ "Are you a developer?"
- Артикль перед профессией нужен и в вопросе (повтор E001)

### E005. have ≠ study/spend (time + activity)
- ❌ "I have one hour learning English"
- ✅ "I study English for one hour" / "I spend one hour learning English"

### E006. Dates: ordinal + the + month name
- ❌ "sixteen day of month" → ✅ "the sixteenth of May"
- Порядковые: first/second/third, далее +th

### E007. Phone numbers — digit by digit
- ❌ "twenty-eight twelve" → ✅ "two-eight, one-two"

### Spelling
- ❌ engener → ✅ engineer
- ❌ english → ✅ English (языки с заглавной)
## W1 D2 — Speaking #1 (2026-05-16)

### E008. Present Continuous → Present Simple (РЕГУЛЯРНОЕ действие)
- ❌ "I'm studying English in my house" → ✅ "I study English at home"
- ❌ "I am living in Poděbrady" → ✅ "I live in Poděbrady"
- Rule: постоянное/регулярное → Present Simple, без -ing
- SRS: D1,3,7,14,30 — главная ошибка, дрилить часто

### E009. at home (не "in my house")
- at home / at work / at school — без артикля

### E010. look for (не "search")
- ❌ "search a new job" → ✅ "look for a new job"

### E011. Блок-фраза
- "I don't know how to say it correctly"
- "How do you say ... in English?"

### E012. like vs would like
- "I like running" (хобби) ≠ "I'd like..." (хотел бы)

### Vocab → Anki
- native language (не "native speaker language")
- grilled food, look for, documentation, articles
- из русского: статья → article, тепло → warm

### E013. Possessives his/her + her name (not "she name")
- ❌ "Her name is Ivan" → ✅ "His name is Ivan"
- ❌ "she name is Mooka" → ✅ "her name is Mooka"
- Rule: his=его / her=её; before noun use his/her, NOT he/she
